Story

There's always more to build.

I grew up taking things apart. Remotes, motors, anything with screws. That curiosity eventually became a direction: mechatronics engineering, where mechanical intuition meets code and circuits. I've designed servo-actuated robotic arms, directed hackathons, and led teams building autonomous systems from scratch. Now I build at the edge of what I know, where robotics meets AI, and ideas become machines that move through the real world. Every project here is something I've built with my own hands.
